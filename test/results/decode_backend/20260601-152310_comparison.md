## Conclusion

- The all-HIP decode experiment saves substantial model-load memory by skipping the Triton fallback dual-storage layout.
- The current prefill-oriented HIP MMQ kernel is too slow for short decode shapes, especially the short decode case.
- Next optimization should be a dedicated small-M decode kernel for M in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}, while keeping the original AWQ layout to avoid dual storage.
